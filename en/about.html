Project Objective
The website septuaginta.com.br was created with the purpose of making God’s Word accessible to everyone, free of charge, simple, and fast.

Free and Open Access
All biblical content can be read, searched, and shared freely, without ads, without data collection, and without the need to register.

Technological Use
Developed using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the project was designed for lightweight performance and excellent compatibility with both mobile devices and computers.

Commitment
We do not seek profit, only to share the Scriptures. If you find errors or wish to contribute improvements, feel free to get in touch.
