At Septuaginta.com.br, we value transparency and respect for our visitors. This document clearly explains our privacy policy.

Information Collection

  • The site does not collect, store, or request any personal data from users.

  • No login, registration, or personal information is required to access the content.

  • All access is completely free and anonymous.

Use of Information

  • Since we do not collect data, we do not use personal information for any purpose.

  • We do not send emails, newsletters, or unsolicited communications.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

  • The site does not use its own cookies to track visitors.

  • Occasionally, there may be cookies from third-party services (such as access statistics or ads, if added in the future). These services follow their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

  • No information is shared with third parties, as no data is collected.

Security

  • Since we do not store user data, there is no risk of personal information leaks.

Changes to This Policy

  • If in the future it becomes necessary to collect any type of data (for example, statistics or personalization), this policy will be updated and visitors will be informed.
